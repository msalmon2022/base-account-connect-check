Safety: This page only calls eth_requestAccounts and eth_chainId. It does not sign messages, create spend permissions, approve tokens, or submit transactions.

If your Base Account does not appear, your current browser may not expose it through the standard window.ethereum interface. This checker is a basic browser-provider test, not a full Base Account SDK integration.
